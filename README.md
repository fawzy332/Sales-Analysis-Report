📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Sales Analysis Dashboard built in Power BI. It was designed to empower C-level executives and sales teams to make data-driven decisions by monitoring key metrics, analyzing seasonal trends, and evaluating channel performance.

The project utilizes a dataset spanning 2011-2013, modeled in a snowflake schema to provide granular insights into transactional data.

    💡 Note on Extra Requirements: This project goes beyond basic visualization. Specific extra requirements regarding logistical risks, forecasting accuracy, and market diversification are addressed in the form of strategic questions within the provided documentation and in the section below.

💡 Key Insights & Recommendations

Based on the analysis, here are the main takeaways:
Insights

    Channel Dominance: Physical Stores drive over 54% of all discounted sales.

    Seasonal Volatility: Significant dips in discounted sales activity occur during April and October.

    Profit Disconnect: Total profit does not always correlate directly with discounted sales volume, indicating a need for tighter discount management.

    Geographic Concentration: Over 50% of continent sales rely on the US market, making revenue vulnerable to local economic downturns.

Strategic Recommendations

    Diversify Markets: Implement a targeted sales strategy to boost market share outside the US.

    Optimize Store Operations: Given that stores represent the majority of sales, focus efficiency efforts there.

    Refine Promotion Strategy: Audit active promotions to ensure they are driving profitable revenue rather than just volume.

❓ Open Action Items & Strategic Questions

Based on the documentation, the following questions must be addressed to ensure logistical and financial success:

    Logistics Risk: How will we mitigate risks of fulfillment delays and customer dissatisfaction caused by warehouse capacity shortages and staffing shortages over the next 6 months?

    Forecasting Accuracy: How can we adjust the historical 2011-2013 shipping forecast data to account for current market growth and volatility, preventing logistical under- or over-planning?

    Promotion Profitability: Are our active promotions actually driving profitable revenue, or just high volume?

    Geographic Vulnerability: What specific strategies will we implement to diversify markets and reduce the current 50%+ reliance on the US market?
